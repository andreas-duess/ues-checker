Privacy Policy

Introduction

This privacy policy explains how Duess Strategies Inc., the consulting practice of Andreas Duess ("I", "me", "my"), handles information when you use ues-checker.duess.com (the "Site"). Use of the Site is also covered by the terms of use.

What Happens to Your UES Files and Text

Nothing leaves your computer. The .docx, .pdf, .txt, and .md files you choose or drop onto the page, and any text you paste, are read and compared locally inside your browser tab using deterministic JavaScript.

  • No file or text is uploaded to any server.
  • No UES content is stored in a database, saved in browser storage, or logged.
  • No text is sent to any AI model, language API, or third-party service.
  • When you clear the fields, close the tab, or refresh the page, your text is gone from memory.

Information You Provide

If you email me at andreas@duess.com for help or feedback, I receive and use only what you choose to send:

  • Your name and email address
  • Any message, question, or details you include

I use this solely to reply to your message and help with your question. I will not add you to a mailing list without asking.

Cookies, Analytics, and Hosting

While your UES files and pasted text never leave your browser, I may use web analytics (including Google Analytics) to measure aggregate visitor counts, page views, and referrals so I know how many people use the tool. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects aggregate usage information under Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser settings.

Cloudflare Pages

The Site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. As the hosting and network provider, Cloudflare may process standard edge connection metadata (such as IP address, browser headers, and performance telemetry) or essential security cookies needed to deliver the page reliably. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy for details.

External Links

The Site links to duess.com and other free USAEDC resources operated by Duess Strategies, and I am not responsible for third-party sites.

Data Retention and Security

Because the checker processes drafts exclusively inside your browser, Duess Strategies never receives or retains your UES files. Any support emails you send are kept only as long as needed to respond and maintain ordinary business records.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information you have sent me by email. To exercise these rights, contact andreas@duess.com. Duess Strategies is based in Canada. If you are not satisfied with my response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your local data protection authority.

Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for adult professionals and is not directed to individuals under 16. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
